2. Page set-up checklist

  1. Fix the Website link. It currently points at jarghaafurniture.com, which does not resolve — every click from Facebook lands on an error. Point it at the live site, or attach that domain to it.
  2. Set the category to Furniture store (add Carpenter as a second category if available).
  3. Add opening hours: Sunday–Friday 7 AM – 7 PM, Saturday closed.
  4. Make the address match the website. The page currently says Talchikhel, Lalitpur 44600 while the site says Gyan Marg, Lalitpur. Pick one and use it on Facebook, Google Business Profile, the website and the directory listings.
  5. Set a @username (e.g. @jarghaafurniture) and turn on the WhatsApp button plus a Messenger greeting and saved replies.
  6. Replace the About text with the copy below.
  7. Claim the directory listings (furniturestoresfinder, interiola) so their address and phone stop drifting.

Post 3 · Care tip

text + one photo · fb-post-espresso-1080.jpg
Three ways to make a wooden wardrobe last longer: 1. Keep it out of direct sunlight — it dries and fades the finish. 2. Wipe with a barely-damp cloth, never a soaked one. 3. Tighten the hinges once a year; loose hinges wear the door edges. Any questions about a piece you already own? Ask below 👇

5. Weekly habits that grow the page

  • Reply within an hour. Furniture buyers message several shops at once; the first useful reply usually wins the job.
  • Ask every customer for a review. Recommendations are the strongest signal on a local page.
  • Post a Reel every week. Short workshop clips reach far more people than photos, for free.
  • Answer comments with a question back — it doubles the number of people who see the post.
  • Pin your best post (a finished piece or the free-measurement offer) to the top of the page.
  • Boost one post a month — the one that got the most messages, targeting Lalitpur and Kathmandu, small budget.
  • Add the page link wherever you can: the website footer (done), Google Business Profile, WhatsApp status, the workshop signboard and your quotation slips.
  • Check Insights monthly and judge posts by messages received, not likes.
