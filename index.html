About Us

Jarghaa Furniture is a family-run workshop in Lalitpur. We make wooden furniture to order — beds, wardrobes and chests of drawers, dining tables, kitchen cabinets and kitchen revamps, sofas, and other woodwork for the home.

The workshop was started by Manoj Jargha Magar, and it still runs the same way: a small team, most of the work done by hand, and every piece finished properly before it leaves. Jargha is our family name, so the name above the door is our own.
