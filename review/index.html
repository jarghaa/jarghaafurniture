Retouched product photos

Each pair shows the original upload on the left and the retouched version on the right. The finished files are in images/products/ at 1400×1200 (7:6), matching the shape of the existing product tiles.

What was changed on every photo: EXIF orientation corrected; keystone (leaning) distortion removed with a projective warp so the carcass reads as a true rectangle; exposure, white balance, contrast and timber colour graded; local contrast lifted so panel detail reads in dark cabinets; edges settled with a soft vignette; then the frame is extended with a deep-emerald (#12251d) soft surround and a hairline gold (#c9a24b) rule, so the set sits together against the site theme.

Two versions of each photo are included. images/products/<name>.jpg (and .webp) is the 1400×1200 framed card for the product grid. images/products/<name>-full.jpg is the same correction without the emerald surround, up to 2000 px, for banners, galleries or a lightbox.

Please note: the third photo was supplied at only 768×1024, so it has been upscaled and is softer than the others — it is worth re-shooting at full resolution.
