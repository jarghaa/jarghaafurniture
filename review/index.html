Studio product photos

Each pair shows the original upload on the left and the retouched version on the right. The finished files are in images/products/: a 1400×1200 (7:6) product card plus square, wide and closer “detail” views rendered from the same correction.

What was changed on every photo: EXIF orientation corrected; the tilt from the camera angle removed with a projective warp so the piece reads as a true rectangle; exposure, white balance, contrast and timber colour graded, with local contrast lifted so panel detail reads in dark cabinets and the piece sharpened.

The background was rebuilt. The room or workshop behind each piece is replaced with a clean, seamless studio sweep — warm ivory falling to stone, with a soft light behind the piece and a contact shadow on the floor — so the furniture sits on a consistent, advertisement-ready backdrop instead of a snapshot of the room it was photographed in.

Several views are rendered from each correction so the page can show the piece straight-on and in closer detail: images/products/<name>.jpg (and .webp) is the 1400×1200 product card; <name>-square.jpg is 1200×1200; <name>-wide.jpg is 1600×900 for banners and the slider; <name>-detail.jpg is a closer 1200×1200 view of the panels; <name>-700.jpg / .webp is the 700×600 hero; and <name>-full.jpg is the full master on the same sweep, up to 2000 px.

Please note: the third photo was supplied at only 768×1024, so it has been upscaled and is softer than the others — it is worth re-shooting at full resolution. The beds and sofas tiles on the product pages still show a placeholder because no source photography for those pieces has been provided yet.
